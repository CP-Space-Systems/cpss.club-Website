Archerfish

Launching December 5, 2026 · Last updated 26.10.05

A pump-fed liquid rocket bringing together CPSS propulsion, structures, and avionics development into a nitrous oxide and ethanol flight vehicle.

Flight Vehicles Liquid Propulsion

Lead: Jasmine Cheng

Background & rationale

[Project background]

Goals

  1. Design, Build, Launch, and Recover Cal Poly Space Systems' first liquid rocket
  2. Launch one of the first collegiate electrically pump fed liquid rockets
  3. Give members an opportunity to develop their skills in avionics design, propulsion testing, structural analysis, high pressure fluid system safety, and more.
