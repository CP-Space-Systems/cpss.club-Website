Prototype development

Engine Throttling

Developing propellant-flow control to throttle a 300 lbf rocket engine between 60% and 100% thrust for the HOP challenge.

Liquid Propulsion Research & Development
Project Leads: Rob Saunders and Gus Chavez
[Project hero image]
[Project image caption]

Design Targets

Nominal engine thrust
300 lbf
Target throttle range
60–100%
Target minimum thrust
180 lbf

Background & Rationale

The HOP challenge requires a vertical takeoff and landing vehicle capable of throttling down to 60% of its designed thrust to hover and descend in a controlled manner. This project focuses on controlling propellant mass flow to achieve that throttle range.

Key Mission Goals

01
Control propellant mass flow to throttle a 300 lbf rocket engine between 60% and 100% thrust, reaching a target minimum thrust of 180 lbf.

Progress & Current Work

Valve analysis and stepper motor selection are complete. Prototyping is in progress.
