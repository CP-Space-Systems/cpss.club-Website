SILL-E

The Student Introductory Liquid Learning Engine program guides student teams through designing, manufacturing, and testing their own small liquid rocket engine with individual support from mentors.

Liquid Propulsion Education

Background & Rationale

SILLE introduces students to liquid rocketry in a supportive environment where no prior experience is required. Teams work with mentors to build a foundational understanding of engine-design variables, manufacturing, and testing procedures.

Working in small groups also helps newer members build connections within CPSS and gain the experience needed to contribute to other projects in the liquids subsystem.

Key Mission Goals

01
Develop future propulsion and test engineers by giving students hands-on experience early in their college careers.
02
Guide student teams through engine analysis, design review, manufacturing, and mentored testing.

Progress & Current Work

The lecture phase is complete, and all groups have completed their initial design work. Teams are now building 3D models and presenting preliminary design reviews.

Manufacturing and testing training are next, with the goal of completing that training by the end of the semester. Hotfire dates depend on the club’s testing schedule.
