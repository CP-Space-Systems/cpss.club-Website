Ablative Engine

Developing a 300lbf liquid bipropellant liquid rocket engine with separate impinging, film, and pintle injectors. This engine will take less time and be much easier to assemble than our previous version, improving testing cadence.

Liquid Propulsion Research & Development
Project Lead: Noah Shefer, Devin Chen
[Project image caption]

Specifications

Target thrust
300 lbf
Target chamber pressure
279 PSI
Target injector stiffness
20%
Target total mass flow
0.64 kg/s
Target O/F ratio
3.5

Background & Rationale

Our on-campus test stand allows us to test several times a week, but not having an engine that is capable of quick-turnarounds has been a limiting factor in our testing cadence. This engine has much fewer bolts and does not use JB Weld, a chemical that takes several days to dry. Another part of this project, the pintle injector, addresses the issues of throttling, a capability CPSS will need for the Collegiate Propulsive Lander Challenge.

Key Mission Goals

01
Develop a film injector.
02
Develop throttling capability.

Progress & Current Work

All math and design has been completed. We are currently ordering parts and in the midst of manufacturing.
