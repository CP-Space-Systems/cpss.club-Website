Certification Rockets

This project is designed to help club members develop skills in designing and building rockets by constructing scratch-built kits to certify L1 or L2 with the Tripoli Association of Rocketry. Members will learn how to use two-part epoxy, laser cutters, 3D printers, and CAD software and have many opportunities to work on experimental rockets.

Background & Rationale

This project is a great opportunity for students with little to no rocketry experience. Members can build their first rocket, gain experience and technical skills in basic design and construction, and gain experience with solid rocket motors.

Key Mission Goals

01
Members will build and launch their own personal rockets built from scratch with the objective of certifying Level 1 or Level 2 with the Tripoli Association of Rocketry.

Progress & Current Work

Members are working on cutting and sanding the body tube, motor mount, fins, and centering rings. The next steps are to epoxy the motor mount and fins to the body tube and attach the shock cord and nose cone. Once everything is put together it will be painted.
