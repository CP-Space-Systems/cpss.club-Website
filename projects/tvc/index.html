Thrust Vector Control

Developing a linear-actuator gimbal for the 300 lbf engine Pollux in preparation to control a lander vehicle.

Flight Controls Research & Development
Project Leads: Gustavo Chavez
Solid Works Assembly of TVC integration to NILE Test Stand

Design Targets

Engine thrust for testing
300 lbf
Target hotfire duration
More than 10 s
Target gimbal angle
7°
Target circular motion
360° — one full circle

Background & Rationale

The lander challenge requires control of the vehicle during flight. The TVC project develops a gimbal that uses linear actuators to change the direction of engine thrust, helping stabilize the vehicle against disturbances such as wind.

Key Mission Goals

01
Statically hotfire a 300 lbf engine for more than 10 seconds.
02
During a 10-second interval, complete one full circular motion while maintaining a 7-degree engine gimbal angle.

Progress & Current Work

The team is preparing for its critical design review and manufacturing the major system components in preparation for testing with linear actuators.

The electronics enclosure and control program are also being developed.
