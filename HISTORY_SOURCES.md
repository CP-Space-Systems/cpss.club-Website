# Homepage history sources

The public summary and six archival images come from the user-supplied “40 Years of CPSS” archive, especially Project Descriptions.docx. The private DOCX research notes are not published or included in the website ZIP. Photos retain their original bytes under descriptive filenames.

Public narrative separates the late-1970s Space Program roots from CPSS's ASI club charter in 1989. EVA welding and LEEF are described as designs/proposals, not completed flights. The title of the supplied folder is not used to assert a precise anniversary.

The archive's April 1993 STS-57 date was a planned launch date. NASA's GAS-450 report confirms the slip to June 21, 1993 and payload retrieval afterward:

https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19940014691
https://ntrs.nasa.gov/archive/nasa/casi.ntrs.nasa.gov/19940014691.pdf
https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/this-week-nasa-history-1st-flight-of-spacehab-june-21-1993/

The notes' proposed STS-64/December 1995 schedule is not presented as a flight outcome. NASA identifies the actual STS-64 launch as September 9, 1994:

https://www.nasa.gov/mission/sts-64/

The homepage gives a brief chronology rather than publishing every archival claim. Additional project history can be expanded when supporting records are supplied.
